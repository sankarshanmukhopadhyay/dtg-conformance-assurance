# Project scope (orientation)

This repository is intended to support **DCAS-aligned implementations** by providing:

- A **common structure** for conformance and assurance claims
- **Machine-readable artifacts** to enable automated validation
- Practical guidance for implementers, assessors, and tooling builders

## In scope

- Claim structures (conformance + assurance)
- Control objectives and risk mappings used by DCAS
- Evidence bundle templates (CSV-first)
- Example claim payloads and verification guidance

## Out of scope

- Issuing authority governance models (left to adopters and their ecosystems)
- Legal opinions, certifications, or regulatory endorsements
- “One true” risk model for all ecosystems

## Design principles

- **Implementation-neutral:** works across different tech stacks and trust registry implementations.
- **Verifier-first:** evidence should be independently checkable and reproducible.
- **Risk-proportionate:** assurance requirements scale with risk and impact.
