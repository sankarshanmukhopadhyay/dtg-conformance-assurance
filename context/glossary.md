# Glossary

- **Assurance Level (AL1–AL4)**: A tiered set of requirements describing what must be true and what evidence must be provided.
- **Claim**: A structured statement asserted by an issuer about conformance/assurance properties.
- **Conformance**: Demonstrable adherence to a specified profile or schema.
- **Profile**: A named bundle of requirements and checks (often versioned).
- **Evidence**: Artifacts that substantiate a claim (test results, audits, logs, attestations).
- **Attestation**: A signed statement asserting facts, sometimes by an independent party.
- **Control**: A requirement intended to reduce risk or ensure integrity of process/outcomes.
- **Registry**: A publication mechanism for claims and related metadata that relying parties can query.
