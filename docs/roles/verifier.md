# Verifier playbook

## Your job-to-be-done
Make reliable accept/reject decisions based on authorization, schema conformance, and status.

## Minimum adoption checklist (DCAS-aligned)
1. Maintain a validation checklist (EV-010).
2. Validate issuer authorization and registry provenance (CO3.4).
3. Validate credential status/revocation signals (CO3.3).
4. Publish conformance claim (examples in `conformance/examples/`).

## Where this shows up in DCAS
- Conformance profile: `conformance/profiles/CP-Verifier.md`
- Mandatory control objectives: CO3.4
- Typical minimum AL: AL2
