# Issuer playbook

## Your job-to-be-done
Issue credentials/claims that are **authorized**, **policy-compliant**, and **auditable**.

## Minimum adoption checklist (DCAS-aligned)
1. Demonstrate onboarding/authorization and registry linkage (EV-007, EV-005).
2. Capture issuance audit trail fields (EV-008).
3. Provide revocation/status mechanism (EV-009).
4. Publish conformance claim (examples in `conformance/examples/`).

## Where this shows up in DCAS
- Conformance profile: `conformance/profiles/CP-Issuer.md`
- Mandatory control objectives: CO3.1, CO3.2, CO3.3
- Typical minimum AL: AL3
